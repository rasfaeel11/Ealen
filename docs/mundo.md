# O mapa do mundo

Descrição do autor (2026-10-30), em inglês, como foi dada. É o prompt do mapa (`mundo` em `art/manifest.json`) e a
referência pra qualquer outro desenho do mundo. Como fato do mundo, ainda precisa ir pro repositório da história
(`historiav2`), junto com os nomes da task 14.

O jogo se passa num canto dele: Xerumbetyba, no interior de Yrandupe, no trecho do Ymonhã entre Kuatiarape e os
baixios de Ybytuguá.

```
WORLD SHAPE: the whole landmass is an UPSIDE-DOWN HEART. The sharp point is at the TOP (north) and is a frozen ice
bridge. The two rounded lobes are at the bottom (south). A long deep ocean channel splits the two lobes from the
south coast all the way up to the ice bridge. Open sea surrounds everything.

North point: "Ponta de Gelo", a narrow bridge of ice and snow joining both lobes. Tiny camps of ice guides.
Central channel: "Mar da Fenda", a dark, deep sea between the lobes, with pirate ships, shipwrecks and glinting
sunken relics.

WEST LOBE: "Yrandupe" (humans and elves)

Northwest: "Zvenoles", dense ancient elven forest and horse meadows.
  ★ "Svyrlogor" (elven seat, deep in the forest)
  ★ "Zorinitsa" (elven star observatory school)
  ★ "Zlatena" (a giant golden tree)

North: "Mboraguá", farmlands and logged forest edges biting into Zvenoles, with stumps along the border.
  ★ "Akaraupe" (capital, big cathedral)
  ★ "Tajyguá" (lumber port on the channel coast)
  "Kyrape" (small farming village)
  "Kururupe" (last village, at the snow line)
  ★ "Muro Velho" (an ancient ruined wall in the far north, just below the ice, at the end of a snowy road)

East coast, facing the channel: "Tanguratyba", a coastal kingdom.
  ★ "Ygarupe" (naval shipyards, north coast)
  ★ "Itamoguá" (big port city, central coast), with ★ "Jekuatyba" (school of magic) inside it
  ★ "Jaguatyba" (royal capital, south of Itamoguá)
  ★ "Ysyrype" (customs city at the river mouth, southern coast)
  "Embyrepe" (old battlefield turned forest park, inland near the western border)

Southwest and center interior: "Xerumbetyba", a land of many rivers, hills and forests.
  The great river "Ymonhã" starts in the western mountains and flows east to the sea at Ysyrype.
  ★ "Ymboripe" (capital, in the western mountains at the river source)
  ★ "Kuatiarape" (university city of libraries, mid-river)
  ★ "Ybytuguá" (old river trade city, downstream, with shallows below it)

EAST LOBE: "Hravgarn" (demons and dragons)

West coast, facing the channel: "Throsvek", a demon kingdom.
  ★ "Brimheld" (port city, under falling ash)

Behind the coast, a long north-south chain of active volcanoes: "Cinzeira". Thick ash clouds blow WEST over
Throsvek; the east slopes are clear. One tallest peak, with wild dragons circling it.
  ★ "Throsvek" (capital with huge glowing forges, at the western foot of the volcanoes)
  Small ruined forge-dungeons inside the mountains.

East of the volcanoes: "Zakhamur", vast open plains of the dragon people, cut by straight roads, canals and mines
climbing the clear east slopes.
  ★ "Garshaaki" (dragon capital, center of the plains)
  Scattered ruined nests and vaults.

South, between the end of the volcanoes and the plains: "Ilgaadur" (the Areal), a vast desert full of ruins, forts,
tombs and dungeon icons.
  A single impossible river, "Idnuur" (the Teimoso), springs from the sand in the middle of the desert and runs
  southwest to the mouth of the channel, with green banks.
  ★ "Vau-de-Osso" (adventurer town at the river ford)

Legend box in a corner: star = important place; skull = dungeon; crossed swords = old battlefield.
```
